Why raygentop's ADP does not improve over the traditional baseline
====================================================================

raygentop's netlist places an unusually heavy demand on IO pins relative
to its logic and H-block requirements. Because IO pads occupy only the
fabric's outer perimeter, their capacity is set by the grid's outer
dimensions alone, independent of how DSP and BRAM tiles are arranged
inside it. This IO demand alone is enough to pin the minimum feasible
fabric footprint to essentially the same size as the traditional
baseline already uses. That, in turn, caps how much room the policy's
placement and aspect-ratio actions have to work with: the array cannot
shrink further no matter where H-blocks are placed, so the
layout-optimization headroom available to the RL formulation is itself
constrained by the benchmark's IO profile before any placement decision
is made. raygentop is consequently the one in-pool benchmark where the
traditional baseline is already close to the floor our action space can
reach.
